Lucy supports individuals navigating low self-esteem, relationship challenges, life transitions, social anxiety, ADHD, and work-life stressors, while welcoming clients from all backgrounds and experiences.

Lucy's approach is warm, collaborative, and person-centred. She believes that clients are the experts of their own experiences and strives to create a supportive space where individuals feel heard, understood, and empowered to explore patterns, deepen self-awareness, and move toward meaningful change. She also incorporates elements of Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) to help clients build practical coping strategies and challenge unhelpful thought patterns.

Lucy's personal experiences navigating social anxiety and ADHD allow her to approach clients with empathy, authenticity, and understanding. She is passionate about helping clients feel less alone in their experiences while fostering reflection, resilience, and self-compassion.

Hi, I'm Akari! I am currently in my third year of the Honours Bachelor of Counselling Psychology program at Georgian College and an Intern Counsellor at Di Fonzo Therapy. Having grown up in different countries and moved schools throughout my childhood, I have learned how our experiences, environments, and cultures shape the way we see the world. These experiences have taught me the importance of understanding each person's unique journey and perspective.

I believe in creating a welcoming, compassionate, and non-judgmental space where individuals feel heard, respected, and valued. My approach is collaborative and person-centred, with a genuine curiosity about each individual's experiences, needs, and goals.

My personal favourite method is Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), while recognizing that counselling should be tailored to each individual. I am passionate about exploring the “why” behind our thoughts, feelings, and behaviours, and how our past experiences can shape the way we navigate the world.

I understand that reaching out for support can be a significant first step, and I appreciate the opportunity to be part of your journey. While the destination is important, I believe the journey that brings us there is just as meaningful.

Adeline Tuyishime Walker

Intern Counsellor

Book A Session

Adeline is an intern therapist dedicated to creating a warm, open, and compassionate environment where individuals feel safe navigating some of life's most difficult experiences. She is currently completing her Honours Bachelor of Counselling Psychology at Georgian College.

Adeline migrated to Canada in 2007 with her immediate family. As an African-Canadian woman who survived the 1994 Rwandan genocide and lived in refugee camps before resettling, she brings a deeply personal understanding of displacement, cultural upheaval, and the challenges of rebuilding a sense of home in a new country. This lived experience shapes her practice: she understands firsthand the difficulty of navigating identity as a person of color, an African immigrant, and a survivor of conflict.

Adeline brings empathy, cultural humility, and a trauma-informed lens to her work with clients. She strives to build spaces of cultural understanding and acceptance, helping clients integrate into a new society without losing connection to who they are, while fostering a genuine sense of belonging. As she often says, a space may not feel exactly like home, but it can become a trusted second home.

Adeline is fluent in Kinyarwanda and English.
